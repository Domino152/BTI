# National Testing Agency URL Directory

Verified on 23 August 2026.

This document lists the main publicly discoverable pages of the National Testing Agency website and the official examination portals linked by NTA. It is intended as a source and information-architecture reference for the NTA Citizen-First hackathon prototype.

## Important scope note

The NTA domain does not expose a working public `sitemap.xml`. Its Notice Board Archive contains more than 1,600 historical entries, and many entries link to separate PDF files. Therefore, this directory covers the stable public pages, student services, organizational pages, action-agenda pages, RTI pages, and official exam portals. It does not individually enumerate every historical notice PDF, tender PDF, corrigendum, answer key, or downloadable attachment.

### Prototype relevance

- The homepage is the main page that your prototype will completely redesign.
- About, governance, RTI, and tender information should remain accessible but should not dominate the student homepage.
- Notice Board content should be filtered by exam, lifecycle stage, date, language, and urgency instead of being shown as one large mixed list.
- The Contact page is an important source for the official exam portal directory.

## 10. Accuracy rules

1. Do not assume that an old NTA page describes the current examination cycle.
2. Verify application dates, correction windows, exam dates, city-intimation releases, admit cards, answer keys, and results on the relevant official exam portal.
3. Add a `sourceUrl` and `sourceCheckedAt` value to every seeded date, notice, resource, or rule.
4. Clearly label synthetic prototype dates, centers, applications, documents, and payments.
5. Avoid claiming that an external logo or link means the prototype is officially approved.
6. Do not reproduce complete official pages or documents. Link to them and summarize only what the student needs.
